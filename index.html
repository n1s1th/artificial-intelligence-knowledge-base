Overview

What this knowledge base covers.

This knowledge base transforms the Wikipedia article on Artificial Intelligence into a structured learning system. Each note is a self-contained unit that connects to the others, forming a coherent path from foundations to the frontier.

Scope

The complete subject of AI: definitions, goals, traditional and learning-based techniques, modern systems, real-world applications, ethical risks, governance responses, philosophical questions, and future trajectories.

Audience

Students, professionals, and curious readers who want a deep, structured understanding of AI — not just surface-level summaries but working knowledge of how the field fits together.

Source & methodology

Primary source: Wikipedia's "Artificial Intelligence" article (~30,000 words). Every major section is mapped to at least one note. Important terminology, concepts, and distinctions are preserved. Where the source is thin, additional context is clearly marked as supplementary research.
